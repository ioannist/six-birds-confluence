Verdict and exact surviving claims

The elementary-flatness/local-confluence equivalence and the normal-form
proposition are correct. Elementary flatness here means fillability of every
distinct-target one-step peak. The equivalence unpacks that definition, adding
the reflexive case; it is not an independent geometric or gauge-theoretic
criterion. The original finite and curated observations survive the repairs.
No title change or weakening of either proved manuscript statement is needed.
Several broad explanatory sentences need qualification in the later paper edit.

The formal library now proves stronger statements:
  * elementary flatness iff local confluence for an arbitrary rewriting relation;
  * termination (reverse-step well-foundedness) implies normalization;
  * termination plus local confluence implies confluence (Newman's lemma);
  * under termination, confluence iff elementary flatness;
  * under weak normalization, confluence iff uniqueness of reachable normal
    forms, where uniqueness alone means at most one, not existence;
  * termination plus elementary flatness gives existence of a unique reachable
    normal form from every start.

Proof inspection

ConfluenceFlat/Core.lean: Reachable is the reflexive-transitive closure of step,
Joinable is existence of a common descendant, and ElementaryPeak excludes equal
targets. The two directions of the core equivalence use exactly these premises.
Originally ARS.step_closed restricted every step to a finite support list. No
proof used that restriction, but the declarations were consequently narrower
than unrestricted abstract rewriting. RewriteSystem now carries just step; all
definitions and proofs quantify over it. ARS remains a finite-support extension
with a coercion, retaining a usable finite representation.

ConfluenceFlat/UniqueNormalForm.lean: a normal form has only itself as a reachable
descendant. Confluence joins two reachable normal forms, so they must be equal.
Normalizing supplies existence independently. These proofs assume neither
termination nor finiteness, and do not hide existence in the uniqueness predicate.

ConfluenceFlat/Termination.lean: well-founded induction is taken on
(b,a) -> step a b, the correct orientation. In Newman's lemma the first steps
lead to b1 and c1. Local confluence gives d reachable from both; induction at
c1 joins c with d at v; induction at b1 joins b with v. The resulting paths
compose to a common descendant of b and c. The converse normal-form criterion
normalizes each branch and invokes uniqueness at the original common ancestor.
All normalizing, local-confluence and termination inputs remain explicit.

review/axioms.lean audits the exported declarations, not just the absence of
local axiom keywords. review/lean_axioms.txt is a fresh output on Lean 4.28.0.
There are no sorry proofs, custom axioms, unsafe computational proof bridges, or
imported Six Birds recognition assumptions. Some declarations use the standard
Lean axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound; others have no axioms.

Computational failures found and repaired

1. two_complex._holonomy_status combined joinability and normal-form outcome-set
   equality. Counterexample: A -> B,C; B -> D,E; C -> D. B and C join at D, yet
   their normal-form sets differ. The peak is trivial under the manuscript's
   definition. Holonomy status and primary defect counts now use joinability
   alone; outcome mismatch remains an independent diagnostic.

2. term_rewriting.generate_left_linear_critical_pairs omitted all i=j overlaps.
   A single rule f(f(x)) -> g(x) has a proper self-overlap at depth one, giving
   f(f(f(x))) -> g(f(x)), f(g(x)). Self-overlaps are now enumerated; trivial root
   overlaps are discarded by branch equality. Variable renamings are quotiented
   when deduplicating schematic branchings.

3. unify resolved only variables at the root, missing indirect occurs cycles.
   The equation f(x,y)=f(g(y),g(x)) previously produced an impossible cyclic
   substitution. Resolution now descends through function arguments before the
   occurs check; returned unifiers are normalized. Cyclic supplied substitution
   environments are rejected.

4. apply_substitution recursively substituted into its own replacements. This
   confuses simultaneous substitution with unifier normalization and loops on
   open-term matches such as x -> x. Substitution is now simultaneous; recursive
   normalization is confined to the unifier. Matching f(x,y) against f(y,a)
   now reconstructs f(y,a), rather than incorrectly replacing y a second time.

5. TRS inputs admitted variable left sides, fresh right-side variables, inconsistent
   function arities, and nonlinear left sides into the explicitly left-linear
   critical-pair generator. Those cases are now rejected at the relevant boundary.
   The one-step rewriting engine still supports valid nonlinear matching; only
   the left-linear generator requires left linearity.

6. Reachability used literal equality of minimal/schematic overlap sources with
   explored states. Thus ab -> x,y at the reachable source zab was missed, as
   were ground instances of symbolic TRS overlaps and overlaps inside contexts.
   reachable_pairs.py now enumerates concrete reachable overlapping redex pairs
   independently from schematic system pairs, recording positions and rule
   indices. reachable_critical_pairs contains those instances. System-level
   source_reachable flags are explicitly scoped to literal sources. Downstream
   counts, holonomy artifacts, completion audits and diagrams use the instances.

7. Bounded searches treated absence of a found join as nonjoinability, or crashed
   when the bounds omitted a branch. A depth-one truncation of the diamond
   a -> b,c; b,c -> d is an explicit false negative. Certification builders now
   require complete reachable and critical-pair closures and fail explicitly
   with an inconclusive-bounds message otherwise. The bounded explorer remains
   available; its graph analysis states its scope and whether the reachable
   closure is certified. Completeness concerns the chosen starts, not every term
   or word in the unrestricted rewriting system. This is a conservative gate:
   even a positive partial witness does not make the full audit complete.

8. TRS outcome mismatch was calculated even for nonterminating closures. It is
   now unset there, matching the finite-ARS diagnostic convention. Primary TRS
   defective-pair counts use nonjoinability, consistently with the string layer.

9. The finite graph representation rejected the empty string although deleting
   string rules are allowed. It now accepts the empty word as a state. Initial
   states exceeding exploration bounds are rejected, instead of silently
   violating those bounds. Termination checking uses an iterative topological
   algorithm, avoiding recursion-limit failures on long finite chains.

10. Completion stages recorded declared rule additions without checking them.
    Their rule sets and starts are now verified to implement exactly the declared
    additions, with the same starts. Existing manual completion examples pass.

11. The freeze helper invented a successful regression result when regression
    was skipped without a receipt. Skipping now requires an explicit result.
    The small regression audit now fails on discrepancies rather than merely
    printing them. The final freeze was produced by actual regression execution.

12. A bare V-shaped peak is not the attaching boundary of a conventional
    topological 2-cell. Artifacts now identify themselves as branching/filler
    presentations, distinguish unfilled peaks from filled branchings, and export
    closed forward/reverse boundary walks only when a filler exists. The existing
    names and two_cells list are retained as the project's branching records;
    they do not assert that an unfilled peak is an attached CW disk.

Required qualifications for the later paper phase (paper left untouched)

paper/sections/03_bare_graph_not_enough.tex and 04_reduction_two_complex.tex:
The full directed graph determines an abstract rewrite relation, its peaks,
joinability and confluence. The code itself computes all of these from that
graph. Therefore claims that it intrinsically contains too little information,
or that elementary flatness cannot be read from it, are false for ARSs. The
valid negative result concerns the directed-cycle and undirected cycle-rank
proxies only. A 2-dimensional presentation packages branching/filler data; it
does not create previously unavailable ARS information. CSM005 is narrowed to
that valid proxy statement without changing the counterexample proposition.

paper/sections/04_reduction_two_complex.tex:
Keep the explicit elementary-holonomy definition as a Boolean fillability
status. It is not group-valued holonomy of an independently specified connection,
nor a theorem about fundamental groups, higher coherence or path homotopy.
Open branching records should be distinguished from actually attached 2-cells.
CSM009 and the regenerated artifacts now expose this distinction.

paper/sections/06_critical_pairs_curvature_generators.tex:
Exact equality of critical-pair and graph-peak signatures holds on the curated
examples, not on arbitrary structured rewriting systems. Disjoint rewrites
commute and produce peaks without critical overlaps; nested variable overlaps
in left-linear TRSs are also handled by compatibility, not by critical pairs.
For example a -> b and c -> d produce a peak at ac with zero critical overlaps.
Likewise f(x) -> g(x) and a -> b give a commuting variable-overlap peak at f(a).
Critical pairs generate the nontrivial overlap obligations, with contextual
instances and substitutions included; they are not the entire local peak set.
The original curated propositions and their numbers remain correct.

paper/sections/09_proved_supported_deferred.tex and appendices:
CSM003 and CSM004 now have actual Lean proofs and can be upgraded in the later
edit. The stronger weak-normalization criterion is also available. The abstract
library is now unrestricted rather than merely a finite-support surrogate.

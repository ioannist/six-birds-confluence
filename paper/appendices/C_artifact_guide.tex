\section{Artifact Guide}
\label{app:artifact-guide}

Every number in the tables of this paper is read from a machine-readable record in the repository. This appendix lists where those records live.

\subsection{Records by family}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Finite abstract rewriting systems: the curated examples are in \code{data/examples/}; the exhaustive enumeration, the counterexample search, and the cycle-proxy comparison are in \code{results/audits/}; peak and filler records are in \code{results/peaks/} and \code{results/plaquettes/}.
    \item String and term rewriting: explored systems are in \code{results/string_rewrite/} and \code{results/term_rewrite/}, and critical-pair analyses, including the lists of reachable critical-pair instances, are in \code{results/critical_pairs/} and \code{results/term_rewrite/}.
    \item Reduction 2-complexes: records of $0$-cells, $1$-cells, and generating branchings, with filled or open status, join witnesses, and closed boundary walks, are in \code{results/two_complex/}; Graphviz diagram sources are in \code{results/diagrams/}.
    \item Completion pairs: the string pair is recorded as a case study in \code{results/case_studies/}; the term pair is recorded through its two explored systems in \code{results/term_rewrite/} and its branchings in \code{results/two_complex/}.
    \item Summary records: the claim-status matrix and the final experiment summary are in \code{results/freeze/}; \code{results/index.json} and \code{results/summary.csv} index all records and are the recommended starting point.
    \item Verification: \code{review/} contains the independent Warshall check, the Lean axiom report, and the record of the last full regression run.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Sections and records}

\Cref{sec:bare-graph-not-enough} uses the cycle-proxy comparison and the counterexample search. \Cref{sec:reduction-two-complex} uses the reduction 2-complex records. \Cref{sec:critical-pairs-curvature} and \Cref{subsec:curated-string-systems,subsec:curated-trs-bridge} use the string, term, and critical-pair records. \Cref{subsec:exhaustive-finite-ars-audit,subsec:boundary-search} use the enumeration and counterexample records and the independent check. \Cref{sec:completion-holonomy} uses the completion and reduction 2-complex records. Earlier planning maps from sections and from table and figure slots to records are kept in \code{paper/notes/}; they predate some of the present tables and figures. The figures of this paper are drawn directly in its LaTeX sources from the examples they depict.
